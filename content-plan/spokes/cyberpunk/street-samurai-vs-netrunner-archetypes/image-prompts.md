# Image prompts — Cyberpunk archetypes: samurai vs netrunner vs corpo

Three prompts for this spoke. Cover follows the Cartographer's Hand spec from `IMAGE-STYLE-GUIDE.md`. Genre accent: **a single neon-pink (`#FF2D9C`) rim light on the character's profile, breaking the palette intentionally**.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A vertical triptych of three half-occluded archetypes, each peeking from behind one letter of the bold word. From left to right: a street samurai in heavy tactical layering with a chrome forearm catching warm light; a netrunner seated in three-quarter view with a cable trailing from a neck-jack and a faint cyan reflection on the cheek; a corpo in a sharply tailored charcoal jacket with a discreet Kiroshi optic in the right eye. Three characters, three silhouettes — heavy, wired, tailored.

Typography:     The single word **ARCHETYPES** in Cinzel weight 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, set in Gold #D4A24C with a subtle Ink #16110D inner shadow. Word occupies roughly 60% of the canvas width, centred horizontally, weighted slightly upward. Each of the three characters is half-occluded by a different stretch of the wordmark — the samurai behind the A, the netrunner behind the T, the corpo behind the second E. The typography clearly in front, characters peeking out.

Kicker line:    "CYBERPUNK SILHOUETTES, SIDE BY SIDE" set in Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, colour Crimson #B23A2A, sitting directly above the bold word. One short line.

Background:     Ink-dark — a dim painted backdrop suggesting an underlit Night City interior, with no signage visible. Patched concrete and shadow. A faint warm bar of Gold light runs horizontally behind the three figures, just enough to lift them from pure Ink.

Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (faint highlight on the corpo's collar), Ink #16110D (background, shadow sides of all three figures), Gold #D4A24C (typography, warm key on the samurai's chrome arm and the corpo's jaw), Crimson #B23A2A (kicker text only). Plus the genre-break neon-pink #FF2D9C — used once, as a thin rim along the samurai's right profile only.

Composition:    Asymmetric within a horizontal arrangement. Three figures stacked from left to right behind the wordmark, each in a different posture — samurai squared up, netrunner seated and leaning back, corpo standing upright. A 60×3px Gold #D4A24C accent rule under the kicker. The neon-pink rim on the samurai only — the other two stay inside the brand palette.

Lighting:       Two-source. Warm Gold key from upper-left raking across all three figures. Neon-pink rim from upper-right hitting only the samurai's silhouette edge. Cool Ink ambient fill everywhere else. Sharp falloff between the three character zones — each lives in a slightly different lighting neighbourhood, the way a party portrait would treat them.

Mood:           A lineup card. Three characters who could sit at the same table without looking like the same character.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, painterly. Frazetta-meets-Syd Mead. The neon-pink reads as paint, not as a digital glow effect.

Negative:       no Hollywood neon-everywhere alley, no purple-orange dual rim cliché, no holographic billboards, no rain, no katana (the samurai's weapon is a slung rifle or sidearm, not a sword), no full chrome face on any figure, no glowing eyes except the corpo's discreet Kiroshi reflex, no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter, no logos, no sparkles, no Cyberpunk 2077 character likeness, no V, no Johnny Silverhand.

### Inline mid-article (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Subject:        A fixer in standing three-quarter view. Late thirties, settled, slightly forward — leaning in to listen. Tailored long burgundy coat over a high-collared deep teal shirt, with a chrome jaw plate etched with a small maker's pattern catching warm light. Statement ring on the right hand, hand half-visible at the lower edge of the frame. Hair slicked back, one chrome eye, the other human. Direct eye contact with the viewer.

Background:     A dim painted interior — suggestion of a back-room bar at evening. Warm Gold lamp light behind the figure, blurred out so the character pops. No signage, no other figures.

Palette:        Burgundy as the coat (a custom warm tone built from Crimson #B23A2A pushed darker and slightly toward purple), deep teal on the shirt (custom — Indigo #2C3E5C pushed warmer), Gold #D4A24C on the chrome jaw and the warm key light, Ink #16110D in the deepest background shadow. No neon-pink — the genre accent stays on the cover.

Composition:    Center-weighted, eye level. Breathing room around the figure. The chrome jaw and the eye line both fall in the upper third — primary focal points. The ringed hand catches a secondary specular at the lower edge. No typography.

Lighting:       Warm key from camera-right, low-angle as if from a lamp. Cool ambient fill from camera-left. The chrome jaw plate picks up the warmest highlight; the burgundy coat eats the light and holds the silhouette.

Mood:           The fixer has just been asked a question and has not answered yet.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, atmospheric. The fabric reads as expensive — the brushwork on the coat is patient and the jaw chrome is rendered with care. Quieter than the cover.

Negative:       no neon, no LED, no chrome-everything, no Hollywood squint, no holographic anything, no Cyberpunk 2077 wardrobe, no second figure visible, no typography, no lens flares, no katana, no spread-out weapons display, no sparkles, no V.

### Inline close (1:1 square, 1080×1080)

Subject:        Detail crop — a netrunner's hand resting on the deck at the hip. The hand is slim, unmarked, mostly flesh, with a single neural cable running from a port on the inside of the wrist up out of frame. The deck itself is a worn matte-black slab with a small Parchment-toned LED panel showing a faint scrolling readout. Two fingers of the hand rest lightly on the deck's edge; the cable curves down and out of frame.

Background:     Negative space — cool Ink shadow with a single faint cyan ambient glow at the upper edge, suggesting a screen above the figure (off-frame).

Palette:        Ink #16110D dominating, with cool cyan-tinted Parchment #F4ECD8 on the deck's LED panel as the brightest specular. Gold #D4A24C as a single warm highlight on the wrist port's metal ring. No Crimson — the image stays cool. No neon-pink.

Composition:    Hand-and-deck in the lower-right third. Strong negative space upper-left. The LED panel and the wrist port catch the brightest specular and give the eye somewhere to land.

Lighting:       Soft cool directional from above, raking across the deck's edge and the wrist. The metal ring on the wrist port reads warm against the cool deck. Deep shadow under the hand.

Mood:           Closing chord. The character has been jacked in for hours. The hand is at rest because the work is happening somewhere else.

Style:          Oil painting, detail crop, visible brushwork on the cable and the skin, smoother rendering on the deck and the LED. Quiet. Painterly.

Negative:       no neon, no LED-blue chrome, no mirror-finish, no glowing veins, no smoke, no purple-orange rim, no typography, no second hand visible, no AI-cliché "hero shot" composition, no Cyberpunk 2077 prop design, no glowing eyes (eyes are not in frame), no sparkles.
