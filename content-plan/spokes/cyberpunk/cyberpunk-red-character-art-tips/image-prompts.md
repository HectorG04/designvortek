# Image prompts — Cyberpunk RED character art: TTRPG brief guide

Three prompts for this spoke. Cover follows the Cartographer's Hand spec from `IMAGE-STYLE-GUIDE.md`. Genre accent: **a single neon-pink (`#FF2D9C`) rim light on the character's profile, breaking the palette intentionally**.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A RED-era solo in three-quarter view, half-occluded by the bold word. Late thirties, weathered. Salvaged tactical layering — patched canvas jacket over a Kevlar plate, a frayed scarf pulled down to the throat, a brass-finished chrome cybernetic arm visible at the lower edge of the frame. Hair cropped short, an old scar trailing from temple to jaw, a single Kiroshi-style cyberoptic implant catching light in the right eye while the left eye stays human. Stoic, settled, scanning off-frame.

Typography:     The single word **RED** in Cinzel weight 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, set in Gold #D4A24C with a subtle Ink #16110D inner shadow as if pressed into parchment. Word occupies roughly 45% of the canvas width, weighted left-of-centre. The solo's profile peeks from behind the right side of the "D" — the typography clearly in front, with the neon-pink rim light skimming his cheekbone behind the letterform.

Kicker line:    "A TTRPG PAINTER'S GUIDE" set in Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, colour Crimson #B23A2A, sitting directly above the bold word. One short line.

Background:     Ink-dark — a Night City alley at 2am in the Time of the Red. Patched concrete and rebar wall behind the figure, fading into pure Ink shadow. No neon signage visible in the frame — the only intentional non-palette colour is the single neon-pink rim from off-camera right.

Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (kicker accent rule, faint highlight on the chrome arm), Ink #16110D (background, jacket shadow side), Gold #D4A24C (typography, warm key on the human side of the face), Crimson #B23A2A (kicker text only). Plus the genre-break neon-pink #FF2D9C — used once, as a thin rim along the right profile and the edge of the chrome arm.

Composition:    Asymmetric. Solo's body angled toward the right third, head turned slightly back toward the camera. Bold RED word weighted left, kicker centred above. A 60×3px Gold #D4A24C accent rule under the kicker. The neon-pink rim threads diagonally down from upper-right, providing the eye path.

Lighting:       Two-source. Warm Gold key from upper-left raking across the human side of the face. Neon-pink rim from upper-right hitting only the silhouette edge — never the front planes. Cool Ink ambient fill everywhere else. Sharp falloff.

Mood:           Four years after the bombs fell. The character is alive, geared, and waiting for someone who is probably not coming back.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, painterly. Frazetta-meets-Syd Mead — period-grounded cyberpunk rather than glossy CD Projekt finish. The neon-pink reads as paint, not as a digital glow effect.

Negative:       no Hollywood neon-everywhere alley, no purple-orange dual rim cliché, no holographic billboards in the background, no rain (RED isn't always raining), no katana, no full chrome face, no glowing eyes (only the one cyberoptic catches light), no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter, no logos, no sparkles, no Cyberpunk 2077 character likeness, no V, no Johnny Silverhand.

### Inline mid-article (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Subject:        A RED-era nomad in standing three-quarter view. Mid-twenties, dust on the jacket, a Snake Nation pattern visible on a headscarf tied loose at the throat. One cybernetic forearm — clean brass-finished prosthetic with an etched maker's mark visible near the wrist. A faded sister's pendant on a leather cord, just visible at the collar. Goggles pushed up onto the forehead, hair tied back. The right eye carries a soft soft warm reflection from an off-frame fire. The left is in shadow.

Background:     Dust haze at golden hour. A vehicle implied off-frame upper-right — a fender catches one warm highlight, the rest dies into dust. Patched canvas and the suggestion of road behind her. No urban signage.

Palette:        Ink #16110D in the deep shadow areas, warm Gold #D4A24C dominating the dust haze and the right side of the face, Crimson #B23A2A on the Snake Nation pattern threads only. Cool Indigo #2C3E5C used once, as the night-coming-on tone in the deepest distance. No neon-pink in this image — the genre accent is restricted to the cover.

Composition:    Center-weighted, eye level. Breathing room around the figure. Pendant catches a small specular highlight at chest level — secondary focal point under the eyes. No typography.

Lighting:       Late afternoon key from camera-left, warm and raking. Cool ambient fill from camera-right. Single dust shaft cuts behind the figure from upper-left, the same way I'd paint a golden hour western — RED's golden hour is dust-heavy too.

Mood:           She's been on the road alone for too long. She is not unhappy about it.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, atmospheric. Quieter than the cover. The dust does most of the atmospheric work.

Negative:       no neon, no LED, no chrome-everything, no rain, no Hollywood squint, no holographic anything, no Cyberpunk 2077 wardrobe, no V, no katana, no spread-out weapons display, no second figure visible, no typography, no lens flares.

### Inline close (1:1 square, 1080×1080)

Subject:        Detail crop — a single hand resting on a holstered sidearm at the hip. The hand is half-chrome, half-flesh, with the prosthetic running from the knuckles up the back of the hand to disappear under a worn canvas jacket cuff. The chrome is brushed brass-finish, not mirror-chrome. A small etched maker's mark sits just below the thumb knuckle. The pistol grip is wrapped in worn rubberised tape; the holster is hand-stitched and weathered. Two fingers of the chrome hand rest on the grip; the flesh thumb is hooked into a belt loop.

Background:     Negative space — warm dust haze in Gold and Ink. The character is implied off-frame upper-right; you see only the edge of the jacket and the belt line.

Palette:        Gold #D4A24C as the dust tone, Ink #16110D in the deepest shadows of the holster and belt, Parchment #F4ECD8 as the faint specular on the brass chrome only, Crimson #B23A2A nowhere — the image stays quiet.

Composition:    Hand-and-holster in the lower-right third. Strong negative space upper-left. The maker's mark on the chrome catches the brightest specular and gives the eye somewhere to land.

Lighting:       Soft warm directional from upper-right, raking across the chrome and the leather. Brass tone reads warm. Leather eats the light. Shadow falls deep and cool under the belt.

Mood:           Closing chord. The character has just walked into a room. The hand is on the gun but the gun is not drawn.

Style:          Oil painting, detail crop, visible brushwork on the leather and the cuff, smoother rendering on the brass chrome. Quiet. Painterly.

Negative:       no neon, no LED-blue chrome, no mirror-finish, no mid-action draw, no spinning, no smoke, no glow, no typography, no second hand visible, no AI-cliché "hero shot" composition, no Cyberpunk 2077 prop design — this is a salvaged RED-era prosthesis.
