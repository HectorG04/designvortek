# Image prompts — Fantasy Character Art (pillar)

Three prompts for the genre-pillar piece. Cover follows the Cartographer's Hand spec from `IMAGE-STYLE-GUIDE.md`. Genre accent: **a gold leaf border ornament with soft glow framing the top-left illuminated initial**.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A scholarly half-elf woman in three-quarter view, half-occluded by the bold word. Late thirties, long swept-back ears just visible past auburn hair pinned at the nape, hazel almond eyes catching warm window light. Wearing a moss-green wool cloak over an ochre tunic, the collar embroidered with a single line of silver script. One hand visible at the lower-right of the canvas, resting flat on the bare grip of a sheathed longsword, fingertips slightly chalk-dusted. The sword is propped point-down beside her, the leather grip-wrap faded red from years of handling. Her expression is quiet, contemplative — not heroic, not grim, just present.

Typography:     The single word **FANTASY** in Cinzel weight 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, set in Gold #D4A24C with subtle Ink #16110D inner shadow as if pressed into parchment with a hot iron. Word occupies roughly 45% of the canvas width, weighted toward the upper-middle third. The "F" is rendered as an illuminated initial — slightly larger than the other letters, with gilt foliate scrollwork curling inside the negative space of the letter and a tiny painted miniature of a candle flame inside the upper loop. The half-elf's face peeks from behind the right side of the "A" and "S" — the typography sits in front of her.

Kicker line:    "A COMPLETE GUIDE FOR PLAYERS GMS AND WRITERS" set in Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, colour Crimson #B23A2A, sitting directly above the bold word. One short line, no wrap.

Background:     Parchment-warm interior — a scholar's study at late afternoon. Deep umber bookshelves in soft shadow, the spines barely legible. A tall window upper-left throws angled warm key light across the figure and onto the floor. A single beeswax candle on a wooden desk in the lower-left is unlit but recently extinguished, with a thin curl of cool smoke rising. Cream-and-parchment paper tones dominate the upper-left third where the gold leaf ornament sits.

Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (page tone behind typography, window-light wall), Ink #16110D (bookshelf shadow, hair shadow, sword scabbard), Gold #D4A24C (typography, gold leaf border ornament, candle highlight, window-light warm key), Crimson #B23A2A (kicker text, faded red grip-wrap on the longsword, single embroidered silver-and-red thread on the collar). Indigo #2C3E5C used once only — as the deepest cool tone in the smoke from the candle.

Composition:    Asymmetric. The illuminated "F" sits in the upper-left third with the gold leaf border ornament hugging it from above and to the left — soft foliate scrollwork, a small painted oak leaf, a tiny gilt vine — all rendered with a faint warm glow as if the gold itself is catching the window light. Half-elf's face offset to the right third, looking past the typography toward the unseen window. Bold FANTASY word weighted left of centre, kicker centred above. A 60×3px Gold #D4A24C accent rule sits just under the kicker. Strong negative space lower-left lets the lit candle and rising smoke sit forward.

Lighting:       Single warm key from upper-left (the unseen window), Ink-deep cool ambient fill on the right side of her face. The gold leaf ornament catches the same warm key with a soft halo glow. The sword grip in the lower-right is in deep shadow, with only a faint warm rim along the top edge of the wrap.

Mood:           Late afternoon in an old library. The scholar has just put down a book she was reading, the candle has just been blown out, and the painter caught the quiet between two thoughts. Not heroic. Not grim. Scholarly-melancholy.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges, warm rim light, deep cool shadows. Frazetta-meets-Penguin-Classics — painterly fantasy with editorial gravitas rather than pulp spectacle. Visible canvas tooth. The gold leaf ornament rendered as actual gilt with hand-painted irregularities, not a digital gradient. Illuminated initial styled after medieval manuscript miniatures, not Disney.

Negative:       no dragons in the background, no glowing sword, no magical particles or sparkles, no purple-orange dual rim light, no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter typography, no AI-cliché epic fantasy lighting, no pointy elf-ear caricature, no chainmail bikini, no battle-axe-raised-to-the-sky pose, no horse in the background, no lens flares, no spinning weapons.

### Inline mid-article (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Subject:        A bearded dwarf, late fifties, three-quarter view. Salt-and-pepper beard forked into two braids tied with small silver clasps, one braid catching warm light, the other in shadow. Heavy bone structure across the brow and cheekbones, deep weather-lines at the corners of the eyes. He is leaning slightly forward, both hands resting on the haft of an axe whose head sits out of frame below the lower edge. He is wearing a dark wool gambeson over an oxblood undertunic, with a brass holy-symbol pendant just visible at the throat — an open palm cradling a small flame. His expression is direct, level, looking at something past the painter's shoulder.

Background:     Forge interior at the end of a working day. Behind him, deep shadow, a faintly suggested anvil, the dull red glow of a banked fire just visible in the lower-right of the background. The forge glow is small — it lights the wall behind him in a soft warm halo but does not reach his face.

Palette:        Ink #16110D dominant across the shadow side of the face and the deep background, warm Gold #D4A24C on the lit side of the beard and the lit cheek, Crimson #B23A2A on the undertunic where the gambeson opens at the throat and on the banked forge glow, Parchment #F4ECD8 only on the brass holy-symbol pendant and the silver beard-clasps catching the brightest specular. No Indigo in this image.

Composition:    Center-weighted, slightly downward angle as if the painter is sitting at a low stool across from him. His face fills the upper two-thirds; the suggestion of the axe haft anchors the lower third. Breathing room around the figure. No typography.

Lighting:       Single warm key from upper-right (the unseen forge entrance, daylight ending), Ink-deep cool fill on the left side of his face. Sharp falloff. The beard catches the key light along the forked braid on the right side. The forge glow behind him does not affect his face — it just shapes the silhouette and warms the negative space.

Mood:           A working dwarf at the end of his day. Not posing. The kind of quiet authority that comes from forty years of doing one thing well. He is about to say something but hasn't yet.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, atmospheric, painterly. Same studio hand as the cover. Frazetta-meets-Rembrandt — strong chiaroscuro, single warm key, deep shadow that holds detail rather than going pure black.

Negative:       no glowing runes on the axe, no magical light on the hands, no horns on the helmet (he isn't wearing one), no helmet at all, no tankard, no exaggerated cartoon-dwarf proportions, no comic-relief expression, no battle pose, no green or blue rim light, no sparkles, no typography, no second figure visible, no lens flares.

### Inline close (1:1 square, 1080×1080)

Subject:        Detail crop — a tiefling's hand resting on the open page of a leather-bound book on a wooden desk. The skin is a saturated warm red, slightly translucent at the fingertips, with a faint inner warmth as if lit from inside. Two fingertips touch the page at the line of script the character was last reading. The nails are dark, almost black, naturally — not painted. A single silver ring on the ring finger, plain band, no stone. Wrist disappears into a cuff of black wool just visible at the edge of the frame. The page beneath the hand is parchment-warm, with handwritten script in dark Ink, slightly smudged near the touched line. No glow on the hand, no flame, no magical effect — just the faint inner warmth of the skin itself.

Background:     Negative space — the desk surface in soft shadow, warm Gold and Ink tones. Beyond the page, the desk falls into shadow. The corner of a second book is just visible in the upper-right.

Palette:        Crimson #B23A2A on the tiefling's skin (saturated, but the brightest specular sits on the knuckles, not pushed beyond local colour), Parchment #F4ECD8 on the page and the silver ring's brightest specular, Gold #D4A24C as the desk-light warm tone, Ink #16110D in the deepest shadows under the hand and inside the wool cuff. No Indigo.

Composition:    Hand and page in the lower-left two-thirds. Strong negative space upper-right. One element dominates: the silver ring, which catches the brightest specular highlight and gives the eye a single resting point.

Lighting:       Soft warm directional from upper-left (an unseen candle just out of frame), raking across the page and the back of the hand. The fingertips closer to the candle source are slightly brighter than the wrist. Shadow falls deep and warm under the palm.

Mood:           Closing chord. The character has just paused in their reading. The painting is over.

Style:          Oil painting, detail crop, visible brushwork on the wool cuff and the desk surface, smoother rendering on the skin and the silver ring. Quiet. Painterly. Trust the negative space.

Negative:       no glowing eyes (no face in frame anyway), no magical fire on the hand, no floating runes, no sparkles, no glowing book, no typography, no second hand visible, no claws or talons (just dark fingernails), no horns visible, no demonic motifs, no AI-cliché tiefling caricature.
