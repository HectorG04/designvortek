# Image prompts — Sci-fi character art commission guide

Three image slots for `/blog/sci-fi-character-art-commission-guide`. Genre accent: a thin cyan circuit-line etched into the serif of one letter on the cover. All prompts assume oil-painting style with visible brushwork — Frazetta + Klimt + medieval illumination, never 3D render or stable-diffusion fantasy defaults.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A spacer in a beat-up padded softsuit, half-occluded behind a giant illuminated serif letter, helmet held in one gloved hand at hip level, head turned three-quarter away. Visible patches and worn stitching on the suit shoulder. Painted in oil, visible brushwork.
Typography:     The single word **STARFIELD** set in Cinzel 700, tight letter-spacing (-0.015em), filling about 45% of the canvas, sitting slightly off-center to the right. The right-side serif of the final "D" has a thin cyan circuit-line (#3FD3E0) etched into it — only that one serif — like a printed-circuit trace that fades into the gold leaf of the letter.
Kicker line:    "A PAINTER'S GUIDE" in Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, color Crimson #B23A2A, sitting above the bold word, short and centered over its leftmost letters.
Background:     Ink-dark. A candlelit study at night reimagined as a cargo bay corner — the painted character stands in a pool of cool fluorescent light against a deeper Ink-dark backdrop. A second warm Gold lamp glow at frame-right suggests a worktable just out of view. Asymmetric composition, painted figure on the left third behind the word, deep negative space on the right.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (the page tone for the typography), Ink #16110D (background), Gold #D4A24C (illumination on the letter, warm rim light on the figure), Crimson #B23A2A (kicker), with the single accent of cyan #3FD3E0 only inside one serif.
Composition:    Headline word right-of-center, painted spacer occupying the left third and partially behind the second letter. The figure's helmet, held at hip, breaks the baseline of the lowest letter. Slight asymmetry — the eye scans from kicker → bold word → figure → out to the negative space on the right. Editorial energy, NYT Magazine cover gravitas.
Lighting:       Cool fluorescent key from above-left on the figure's face (the sci-fi temperature), warm Gold candlelight rim from frame-right (the brand temperature). The letter itself is lit as if by warm parchment light. Deep ink shadows fill the negative space.
Mood:           A working spacer between shifts, painted as a manuscript illumination — the future seen through the cartographer's hand. Not action, not heroic posture. Quiet, observed, mid-thought.
Style:          oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges where light wraps, hard edges only on suit panels and helmet trim. Frazetta + Klimt + medieval illumination. The illuminated letter feels like gold-leafed parchment, not digital text. The figure feels painted, not rendered.
Negative:       no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter, no AI-cliché purple-orange dual rim light, no lens flares, no sparkles, no stock heroic poses, no helmet on the head, no laser blasts, no magical particles, no chrome filigree, no Halo-style "iconic helmet shot," no neon pink or cyberpunk neon palette, no over-rendered armor, no beauty-filtered face, no stable-diffusion fantasy painting defaults

### Inline mid-article image (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Subject:        A vesk-coded reptilian humanoid alien in a softsuit, head and shoulders, three-quarter view. Skin in muted olive and slate tones with subtle scaling along the jaw and temple. A vestigial dorsal ridge runs from the back of the skull down into the suit collar. Eyes amber, slit-pupiled, focused off-frame to the right as if listening. Single small piece of jewelry — a thin bone-and-metal pendant at the throat, just visible above the suit's harness.
Typography:     None.
Background:     Deep Ink-dark with the faintest warm haze in the lower-right corner — a freighter bulkhead suggested, not described. Strong negative space behind the figure's head.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (subtle warm haze), Ink #16110D (background), Gold #D4A24C (rim light along the jaw and shoulder), muted olive and slate for the skin. No Crimson, no Indigo on this image.
Composition:    Centered subject, head occupying the upper 60% of the frame, shoulders cutting the lower edge. Breathing room above the head. The viewer's eye lands on the eyes first, then drifts down to the jaw scaling, then to the pendant.
Lighting:       Single warm Gold key from frame-right, cool secondary fill at maybe 10% intensity from frame-left. Deep shadow on the left side of the face. The pendant catches a small specular of the key light.
Mood:           Quiet, attentive, alien-but-readable. The portrait equivalent of a character noticing something across the room. Painted as if the model sat for the portrait in a candlelit cabin between watch shifts.
Style:          oil painting, visible brushwork especially around the skin texture, soft edges on the cheek and jaw where light wraps, harder edges on the suit collar and pendant. Frazetta + Klimt warmth, with the species departures from human held to three: scaling, dorsal ridge, slit pupils.
Negative:       no rubber-mask look, no five-departures-from-human alien design, no neon glow, no exposed cybernetics, no helmet, no weapon, no sci-fi background clutter, no AI-cliché lighting, no symmetric face composition, no beauty-filter skin smoothing, no glowing eyes

### Inline close image (1:1 square, 1080×1080)

Subject:        A close detail crop of a single gloved hand resting on a worn helmet that sits on a metal surface. The hand is human, the glove is soft padded sci-fi softsuit material with visible stitching along the seams and a small embroidered unit patch on the cuff. The helmet visor is down and shows a faint reflection of a warm Gold lamp.
Typography:     None.
Background:     Cream #FAF4E2 surface (the metal table reads warm under the brand light), Ink #16110D negative space behind the helmet at frame-top, a hint of warm Gold lamp glow at frame-right edge.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8, Ink #16110D, Gold #D4A24C (reflection in the visor and rim on the helmet curve), Cream #FAF4E2 (the table surface), muted slate of the helmet body.
Composition:    Detail crop. Hand and helmet fill about 70% of the frame, weighted to the lower-left. Strong negative space upper-right. The eye lands on the reflected Gold spark in the visor first, then follows the hand back to the unit patch on the cuff.
Lighting:       Single warm Gold key from frame-right, casting the hand and helmet's right edge in warm light and leaving the left side in soft Ink shadow. The visor's reflection of the lamp is the visual chord that closes the article.
Mood:           Closing chord. The painter has finished. The character's day is over. The helmet sits where it sits because the spacer just took it off and hasn't picked it back up.
Style:          oil painting, visible brushwork on the glove stitching and the helmet edge wear, soft edges on the table surface, single sharp specular in the visor reflection. The whole image is quiet — the visual equivalent of a held breath.
Negative:       no full figure, no face visible, no typography, no neon, no sci-fi cliché backlighting, no glowing helmet HUD, no weapons, no chrome filigree, no overworked detail, no AI-cliché purple-orange lighting, no lens flare, no sparkles, no decorative scrollwork
