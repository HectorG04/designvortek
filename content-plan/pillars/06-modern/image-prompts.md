# Image prompts — Modern Character Art (pillar)

Three slots, model-agnostic. Cover is text-first and integrated with painted subject. Inline pieces carry no typography. Cover accent for this genre: a streetlight sodium-yellow glow behind the painted figure.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A young woman painted in three-quarter view from chest up, wearing a long second-hand black wool coat with the collar turned up, a grey ribbed Henley underneath, a thin silver chain at the throat. Hair is slightly damp, tucked behind one ear. Her face is half-lit from the right by a sodium-yellow streetlight just out of frame; the left half of her face falls into deep ink-coloured shadow. Visible brushwork on the coat and on the skin. She is half-occluded by the central capital letter — the letter sits over the right side of her body so we can read her face and one shoulder but not her hands.

Typography:     The single word **MUNDANE**, set in Cinzel weight 700, all caps, letter-spacing -0.015em, painted as if gilded into a sheet of brushed metal — colour Gold #D4A24C with a worn matte texture, scale roughly 42% of the canvas height, sitting slightly left-of-centre and rising from the lower third up through the middle.

Kicker line:    "A PLAYER'S GUIDE TO MODERN PORTRAITS" — Inter uppercase 600, letter-spacing 0.15em, colour Crimson #B23A2A, set above the bold word.

Background:     Ink-dark — a Chicago-style alley wall behind her, suggested in soft brushy textures, brick and damp concrete reading more as tone than as detail. A sodium streetlight casts a warm yellow corona on the wall behind her head and shoulder, the cover's required genre accent.

Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8, Ink #16110D, Gold #D4A24C, Crimson #B23A2A, plus the sodium-yellow glow as an accent halo only.

Composition:    Asymmetric. The painted figure occupies the right two-thirds; the kicker + headline + gold accent line stack left-of-centre, with the headline overlapping the figure's coat. The 60×3px gold accent line sits directly under the kicker, colour #D4A24C. The sodium-yellow streetlight glow forms a halo around the figure's head, just bright enough to read against the ink-dark wall.

Lighting:       Single warm sodium source from upper-right, behind the figure, wrapping around her silhouette as a rim light. Cool ambient bounce fills the shadow side at very low intensity. No fill light on the face.

Mood:           A modern character standing under a streetlamp at 11pm, half a block from the door she's about to walk through. The painting is paused on the half-second before she moves.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges where shadow meets skin, sharper edges on the collar and the silver chain. Frazetta + Klimt + medieval illumination, but pulled into a contemporary subject. Gilded typography sits ON the scene, not in front of it.

Negative:       no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter, no AI-cliché purple-orange dual rim light, no lens flares, no sparkles, no stock fantasy poses, no fangs visible, no obvious supernatural cues, no neon pink, no purple, no katanas, no leather trench coats with tactical webbing, no Matrix sunglasses, no rain droplets on the camera lens, no bokeh string-light backgrounds, no faux-parchment scroll borders.

### Inline mid-article (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Subject:        A man in his late thirties seated at a small wooden desk, photographed from the chest up at a slight angle. He wears a charcoal suit, sharply cut, white shirt open at the collar, no tie. His hands rest on the desk in front of him — the fingertips of his right hand are stained slightly grey-blue, the colour of dried modelling primer. A single brass desk lamp throws warm light across the desk and onto the lower half of his face; his eyes are in soft shadow. Behind him, just visible at the edge of the frame, the corner of a balsa-wood architectural model.

Typography:     None.

Background:     Warm ink-dark. A study or home office at night, suggested in soft painterly blocks — a bookshelf edge, a window with the city blurred to streaks of cold blue beyond the warm interior. The contrast between the warm desk lamp and the cold window is the emotional core of the piece.

Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 in the lamp-lit highlights, Ink #16110D in the shadows, a touch of Indigo #2C3E5C in the window beyond. No crimson, no gold accents — restraint.

Composition:    Centered figure, slight tilt. The painted fingers are the focal point, not the face. Strong negative space upper-right. Eye travels from lamp → hands → eyes → window → back.

Lighting:       Single warm source (desk lamp) from lower-left, cold rim from window upper-right at much lower intensity. Hard contrast on the suit lapel, soft falloff on the skin.

Mood:           A contemporary character mid-thought. Not in distress, not in triumph. Working. The kind of late-evening quiet where the city has gone soft around the edges and the desk lamp is the only thing still sharp.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges on the suit fabric, sharper edges on the stained fingertips, painterly atmospheric falloff in the background. Editorial portrait energy — feels like a feature spread, not a stock author photo.

Negative:       no typography, no logos, no laptops with glowing apple icons, no smartphone in hand, no obvious "writer at desk" props (no whiskey glass, no typewriter, no fountain pen close-up), no cigarette smoke, no AI-cliché bokeh string lights, no faux-Rembrandt theatrical lighting, no purple-orange dual rim, no fantasy elements at all.

### Inline close (1:1 square, 1080×1080)

Subject:        A close detail crop. A woman's hands resting on the edge of a desk in warm pooled light. The left hand is empty, fingers slightly curled. The right hand holds a single small object — a folded scrap of paper, the corner of a photograph, or a key — not fully revealed, partially shadowed by the curl of her fingers. A thin silver chain ring is visible on her index finger. The skin shows lived-in texture: a small healed scar across one knuckle, the faint ink of a phone number written on the heel of her thumb three days ago and not fully washed off.

Typography:     None.

Background:     Deep ink-dark, almost black, with a soft warm halo where the desk lamp's light pools onto the wood grain. Strong negative space — the hands and the small object are the entire painting; everything else falls away.

Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 in the highlights on the skin and the warm pool, Ink #16110D for the shadow field, Gold #D4A24C in the very faintest reflection on the silver chain and along the desk edge. Crimson is absent from this image.

Composition:    Hands centered slightly lower-right, lamp pool falling from upper-left. The held object is the question the image is asking — what is it, who gave it to her, what is she about to do with it. Strong asymmetric framing; lots of dark space upper-left.

Lighting:       Single warm desk-lamp source from upper-left, falling at a low angle across the hands. Skin catches the warm light; the silver chain catches a faint sub-highlight of gold. The held object is half in light, half in shadow.

Mood:           A closing chord. The end of the article should leave the reader with one unanswered question, and these hands are the question. Quiet, charged, paused.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges in the skin, harder edges where the desk meets the light. Detail crop energy — feels like the closing shot of a film, not a flourish.

Negative:       no typography, no rings other than the silver one specified, no nail polish (paint it as bare skin), no tattoos visible, no jewellery overload, no phones or modern tech props beyond the held object, no fantasy elements (no rune-engraved rings, no glowing symbols), no AI-cliché diamond-cut bokeh, no purple-orange rim, no over-saturated colour, no over-rendered photoreal skin.
