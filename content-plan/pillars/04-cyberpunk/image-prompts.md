# Image prompts — Cyberpunk Character Art Commission Guide

Three prompts for the genre-pillar piece. Cover follows the Cartographer's Hand spec from `IMAGE-STYLE-GUIDE.md`. Genre accent: **a single neon-pink (#FF2D9C) rim light on the character's profile, breaking the brand palette intentionally**.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A street-samurai's face in three-quarter view, half-occluded by the bold word. Late-2040s register — close-cropped dark hair, a sub-dermal port at the temple catching cold light, brushed-steel jaw plate covering the lower right quadrant of the face from cheekbone to chin, one cyberoptic eye reading a fraction colder than the natural left eye. Skin shows wear: a faint scar through the eyebrow, a healing nick along the cheekbone where the metal meets flesh. Expression is still, the head turned just enough that the chrome catches the sodium key. Shoulders just visible at the lower edge — a high collar of a worn armoured jacket, a single frayed thread.

Typography:     The single word **NEON** in Cinzel weight 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, set in Gold #D4A24C with a subtle Ink #16110D inner shadow as if struck into hot metal. Word occupies roughly 40% of the canvas width, weighted left-of-centre and toward the upper third. The character's profile peeks from behind the right edge of the "N" and "O" — the typography clearly sits in front of him, sodium-gold against the deep interior shadow.

Kicker line:    "A PAINTER'S GUIDE TO THE CHROME" set in Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, colour Crimson #B23A2A, sitting directly above the bold word. One short line, no wrap.

Background:     Ink-dark interior — the corner of a Night City back-alley garage or low-rent ripperdoc clinic at the end of the day. Deep umber and ink-black, a hint of a steel shelf or workbench out of focus behind him. No visible neon signs, no rain, no street. The atmosphere is dense and quiet, not busy. A faint cool ambient fluorescent off-frame upper-left fills the shadow side.

Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (kicker accent rule and a faint gold-leaf detail only), Ink #16110D (background and shadow side of face), Gold #D4A24C (typography, sodium key on cheek and chrome plate, warm specular on the metal), Crimson #B23A2A (kicker text only). Genre accent: a single neon-pink (#FF2D9C) rim light tracing the very edge of his profile from temple down the line of the jaw plate to the chin — this is the one intentional palette break, localised to the silhouette only.

Composition:    Asymmetric. Character's face offset to the right third, looking past the typography off-frame upper-left, eyes catching the cool ambient. Bold NEON word weighted left, kicker centred above. A 60×3px Gold #D4A24C accent rule under the kicker. The neon-pink rim runs as a thin clean line down the right side of his face — visible against the deep Ink background, invisible where it crosses the gold typography.

Lighting:       Warm sodium key (Gold #D4A24C) raking up from below-right onto the chrome jaw plate and the lower face. Cool cyan-tinged fluorescent ambient fill from upper-left on the shadow side of the face. The neon-pink rim is a separate localised source off-frame right — it touches only the profile edge. The chrome catches the sodium sharply as a bright specular; the skin beside it absorbs the same light softly.

Mood:           Late shift. He has just sat down for the first time in eight hours. The painter caught him in the moment before he closes his eyes.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges on skin, sharper rendering on chrome and on the sub-dermal port. Syd Mead industrial design DNA filtered through a Ghost in the Shell OVA palette, not a 2077 marketing render. Visible canvas tooth on the matte surfaces.

Negative:       no rain, no wet streets, no holographic billboards, no busy neon signs in background, no mirrored sunglasses, no purple-orange dual rim light, no chromatic-aberration filter overlay, no scanlines over the whole image, no katanas, no glowing eyes, no LED-blue chrome, no anime-mecha proportions, no blackletter, no skulls, no AI-cliché "cyberpunk character poster" lens flares.

### Inline mid-article (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Subject:        A Shadowrun netrunner, late twenties, seated in a high-backed deckchair, body slack. Her eyes are closed but her face is doing something subtle the rest of her body can't — a half-smile that doesn't reach the mouth's corners, a slight asymmetry around the eyebrows as if she's listening to something only she can hear. A trodes-band runs across her forehead just above the eyebrows, with a single cable trailing off-frame lower-right toward an unseen deck. One dermal port behind her left ear is just visible under cropped hair. No chrome on her hands or arms — this character is hidden cyberware only, the augmentation lives inside her skull. Loose dark hoodie, sleeves pulled over her hands.

Background:     The character's chair sits in deep shadow. A single monitor off-frame upper-left throws cold cyan glow across her face and the trodes band. Beyond her, the room dies into darkness — only the faint suggestion of a wall, the corner of a desk, the back of an unused chair. The light is the room. The room itself is barely painted.

Palette:        Ink #16110D dominant across the deep shadow background and the hoodie folds. Cool desaturated cyan as the monitor key light across her face and forehead. A single warm Gold #D4A24C accent only in one small place — the soft glow of a status LED on the cable trailing off-frame, barely visible. No pink anywhere in this image — it is intentionally calm.

Composition:    Center-weighted, slight downward angle as if the painter is standing beside her chair looking down at the face. The trodes band sits across the upper third. Her closed eyes are the focal point. The cable runs out of the lower right. Negative space dominates the upper half. No typography.

Lighting:       Single cool monitor source from upper-left, low-intensity. Hard falloff. Her face is the brightest element in the painting; the rest is at least two stops darker. The cyan barely warms the skin; it stays cold and slightly green at the highlights, classic LCD-glow.

Mood:           She is not in the room. The painter is.

Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, atmospheric, painterly. Same studio hand as the cover. Strong restraint — the painting trusts the viewer to fill in the cyberspace work without showing any of it.

Negative:       no holograms, no glowing brain, no obvious wireframes, no Matrix-style green code, no LED stripes on her clothing, no chrome cyberware visible, no mirrored sunglasses, no second character, no typography, no chromatic aberration, no scanline overlay, no purple-orange lighting.

### Inline close (1:1 square, 1080×1080)

Subject:        Detail crop — a chrome forearm and hand resting on the edge of a workbench. The hand is brushed steel with matte titanium accents, four fingers and an articulated thumb, knuckle plates showing fine scratches and a small dent on the back of the index finger. At the wrist, the chrome meets a wrist seam: a thin band of skin-on-metal with a faint scar lip running around the join, a millimetre of pink-flush inflammation where the body hasn't entirely accepted the metal. Above the seam, a few centimetres of pale forearm before the canvas ends. The hand rests palm-down, fingers relaxed but slightly curled. A single small brass mounting screw is visible at the wrist joint.

Background:     Negative space — soft warm shadow falling away from the hand. The workbench surface is roughly suggested in dust-warm umber, a hint of a tool just out of focus, nothing else.

Palette:        Brushed-steel grey as the dominant tone across the chrome, Ink #16110D in the deepest shadows under the hand and at the wrist seam, Gold #D4A24C as a warm specular highlight along the top of the knuckle plates and the inside curve of the thumb, Parchment #F4ECD8 as the faintest cool highlight on one knuckle catching cyan ambient. Crimson #B23A2A appears only as the millimetre of inflammation at the wrist seam — the single warmest, most human note in the frame.

Composition:    Hand placed across the lower two-thirds, fingers reaching slightly into the lower-right corner, wrist seam in the centre of the canvas as the natural focal point. Strong negative space upper-left. One element dominates: the wrist seam where skin meets metal.

Lighting:       Soft warm directional from upper-right, raking across the chrome. The knuckle plates catch the brightest specular. The skin above the seam reads slightly cool by contrast. Shadow falls deep and quiet under the hand.

Mood:           Closing chord. The chrome is the surface, the seam is the story.

Style:          Oil painting, detail crop, visible brushwork on the skin, smoother rendering on the metal, sharpest rendering on the small brass screw. Quiet. Painterly. Trust the negative space.

Negative:       no cables coming out of the wrist, no glowing components, no LED stripes, no overly-stylised mecha-finger proportions, no mirror-polish chrome (matte and brushed only), no chromatic aberration, no typography, no second hand visible, no dramatic Hollywood angle, no AI-cliché "cyberpunk hand" hero-shot composition.
