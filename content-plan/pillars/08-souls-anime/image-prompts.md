# Image prompts — Souls & Anime Fan Art Commission Guide (pillar)

Three slots: cover (16:9), inline mid-article (4:5), inline close (1:1). Cover uses the souls-anime accent: cell-shaded line + a small katana-grip tassel hanging from one letter's descender.

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A painted figure in a low hood and weathered plate armor, three-quarter view, half-occluded by the bold word. The figure is rendered as a painterly Elden Ring-coded Tarnished — gilded edge along the cheekbone and pauldron, deep cool shadow on the off-side. Mounted to one letter's descender, a small wrapped katana grip with a frayed dark-red tassel hanging two inches below the baseline, hand-painted, not graphic.
Typography:     The word **"TRIBUTE"** set in Cinzel 700, ink-deep `#16110D`, letter-spacing -0.015em, occupying roughly 42% of the canvas width, left-of-center. One letter (the "U") has the small painted katana tassel hanging from its descender. A faint cell-shaded ink contour, single-weight, edges one stroke of the "T" — a subtle anime line-art accent integrated into the otherwise oil-painted scene.
Kicker line:    "A FOUNDER'S FIELD GUIDE" set in Inter 600 uppercase, letter-spacing 0.15em, color Crimson `#B23A2A`, placed above the bold word.
Background:     Parchment-warm `#F4ECD8` page tone with a soft gradient suggesting late-afternoon light from the upper-right. Faint cream `#FAF4E2` variation in the lower-left to keep the composition asymmetric. No texture overlay, no fake aged-paper edges.
Palette:        Parchment `#F4ECD8`, Ink `#16110D`, Gold `#D4A24C` (rim light on the armor and a 60×3px accent line under the kicker), Crimson `#B23A2A` (kicker text and the tassel cord), Cream `#FAF4E2`.
Composition:    Slight asymmetric layout. The bold word sits left-of-center on the upper two-thirds. The painted figure peeks from behind the right edge of the word, occluded from chin to chest by the typography, eyes catching the gold rim light just above the cap-height. The tassel hangs from the "U" descender into the lower-third negative space. The 60×3px gold accent line `#D4A24C` sits directly under the kicker.
Lighting:       Single warm directional key from upper-right at gold-hour angle (Elden Ring-coded), cool ambient fill from the parchment background. Long shadow on the figure's off-side, gilded edge on the key side. Soft falloff, no hard rim.
Mood:           A candlelit study at the end of a long working day, the painter has just finished a tribute piece and propped it against the desk. Restrained. Grown-up. The image asks one question: what does fan art look like when it's done right?
Style:          Oil painting with visible brushwork on the figure, soft edges, a single hand-drawn cell-shaded ink contour as the genre accent. Lineage: Frazetta + Klimt + medieval illumination, with one faint nod to a modern anime cover line.
Negative:       no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter, no AI-cliché purple-orange dual rim lighting, no dragons, no lens flares, no sparkles, no stock fantasy poses, no neon, no anime-cliché big-eyes face, no Pinterest-style fan-art pastiche, no franchise-specific armor (no Malenia helmet, no Geralt swords, no Genshin gacha-art rendering — original studio composition only), no pure black `#000` or pure white `#FFF`, no drop shadows on the typography.
