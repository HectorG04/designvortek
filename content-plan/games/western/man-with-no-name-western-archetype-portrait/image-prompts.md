# Image prompts — The Man With No Name: the western archetype that won't die

Genre accent: **Western** — a dust-mote light shaft cuts diagonally across the cover, behind the typography.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A drifter in three-quarter view, wearing a heavy striped poncho draped asymmetrically off one shoulder, a wide-brimmed wool hat sitting low so the upper face goes into deep shadow with only a strong squint reading from under the brim. A thin cheroot pinched in the side of the mouth, a soft thread of smoke rising. Three-day stubble. The character's bone structure is intentionally not Clint Eastwood — broader cheekbones, different jawline — but the silhouette signals the archetype immediately.
Typography:     The bold word "NAMELESS" set in Cinzel 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, in Ink #16110D with a soft warm dust haze across the lower third of the letters. Word takes ~45% of canvas, sits slightly right of centre. One letter (the L) has a faint vertical hairline crack like an aged poster.
Kicker line:    "AN ARCHETYPE PORTRAIT" — Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, in Crimson #B23A2A, sitting above the bold word. 60×3px gold accent line in #D4A24C beneath the kicker.
Background:     Parchment-warm — a wide western horizon in late golden hour, no specific landmarks, atmospheric perspective falling into warm hazy distance. A diagonal dust-mote shaft cuts from upper-left across the cover.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8, Ink #16110D, Gold #D4A24C, Crimson #B23A2A, with warm amber and burnt sienna across the horizon and a striped poncho carrying Crimson and a muted dust ochre.
Composition:    Asymmetric. Bold word right-of-centre; the drifter sits left, the hat brim and one curl of cheroot smoke crossing in front of the leading edge of the typography. The dust-mote shaft passes behind the bold word.
Lighting:       Single warm golden-hour source from frame-left low. Deep cool shadow under the hat brim across the upper face; warm rim light catching the cheekbone and the curl of poncho fabric.
Mood:           A character who has been walking since morning, has just stopped, and is looking at something off-frame the viewer cannot see.
Style:          oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges, Frazetta + Sergio Leone freeze-frame + Andrew Wyeth horizon, faces rendered with period-photograph weight
Negative:       no blackletter, no wax seals, no skulls, no AI-cliché purple-orange dual rim light, no glowing eyes, no recognisable likeness of Clint Eastwood (different bone structure required), no caped or heroic poncho flow, no full-face hat shadow that obscures the squint, no smirk, no spinning revolver, no horse in frame

### Inline mid-article (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Subject:        A drifter shoulders-up in three-quarter view, hat tilted forward, the upper half of the face entirely in shadow except for a strong squint and the catchlight in one eye. The lower face is lit by harsh noon sun — three-day stubble, closed mouth slightly weighted, a faint shine on the lower lip. A piece of poncho fringe just visible at the shoulder edge. The character is again deliberately not Eastwood — different cheekbones, different jawline.
Typography:     None.
Background:     Warm dusty mid-distance, no specific landscape, a single thin band of cool shadow at the lower edge suggesting the character is standing in their own shadow at noon.
Palette:        Warm ochre and burnt sienna in the background, Ink #16110D in the hat-brim shadow, warm flesh tones with deep cool shadow on the upper face, a single thread of Crimson #B23A2A in a poncho stripe.
Composition:    Centred subject, the head tilted just slightly down so the hat brim does the work of cutting the upper face into shadow. Generous breathing room above the hat. The lit lower face is the brightest area on the canvas.
Lighting:       Single hard noon source from directly above and slightly forward. The hat brim casts a sharp shadow across the upper face. No fill light.
Mood:           Noon. Heat. The character has been standing in this exact spot longer than is comfortable, and is in no hurry to move.
Style:          oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges, harsh-sun portrait register, painted with the same gravity as a Mathew Brady tintype
Negative:       no typography, no glowing eyes, no smirk, no Eastwood likeness, no anime expression, no AI-cliché beauty-filtered skin, no purple-orange rim light, no exaggerated cheroot smoke, no recognisable mountain landmarks
