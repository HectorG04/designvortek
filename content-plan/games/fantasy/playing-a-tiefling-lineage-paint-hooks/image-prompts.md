Subject:        A painted tiefling portrait in three-quarter profile, ram-curl charcoal horns curving from the temples, brick-red skin with warm undertones, gold eyes catching a single candle source. Kicker line:    "A PLAYER'S LINEAGE GUIDE" — Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, in Crimson #B23A2A, sitting above the bold word. 60×3px gold accent line in #D4A24C beneath the kicker.
Background:     Ink-dark — a candlelit study at night, faint suggestion of a manuscript on a desk in the deeper shadow. Warm pool of light around the face, deep black falling off to the right side of the canvas.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8, Ink #16110D, Gold #D4A24C, Crimson #B23A2A
Composition:    Asymmetric. Bold word left-of-centre; the tiefling's face emerges from behind the right edge of the word, one horn arcing up behind the T. Gilt-leaf border ornament in the top-left corner as the genre accent. Eye is the single brightest point on the canvas.
Lighting:       Single warm candle source from frame-right, falling on the face and the front edge of the horn. Deep cool shadow on the opposite side.
Mood:           Late-night study, the painter has been working past midnight; the subject is poised, weary, intelligent.
Style:          oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges, Frazetta + Klimt + medieval illumination, faces painted from observation rather than reference fantasy art
Negative:       no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter, no AI-cliché purple-orange dual rim light, no dragons, no lens flares, no sparkles, no stock fantasy poses, no snarling open mouth, no glowing red eyes, no Halloween-costume reads, no horns extending past the canvas edge
