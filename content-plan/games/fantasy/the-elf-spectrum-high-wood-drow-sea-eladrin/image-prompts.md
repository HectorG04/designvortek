### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A composite cover — the painted profile of five different elves arranged in a subtle horizontal frieze behind the typography, each one painted as a half-silhouette so the reader catches the differences in ear shape, skin tone, and clothing register. From left to right: a pale high elf in cool ivory with silver hair, a sun-warm wood elf with copper braids, an obsidian-skinned drow with white hair, a pale aquamarine sea elf with coral-red hair, and an autumn eladrin with russet skin. They are all partially occluded by the bold word in the foreground.
Typography:     The bold word "SPECTRUM" set in Cinzel 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, in Ink #16110D. Word takes ~50% of canvas, sits slightly right of centre. The S and the M are illuminated with a soft Gold #D4A24C inner glow as if backlit by candle.
Kicker line:    "FIVE WAYS TO PAINT AN ELF" — Inter uppercase 600, 0.15em letter-spacing, in Crimson #B23A2A, sitting above the bold word. 60×3px gold accent line in #D4A24C beneath the kicker.
Background:     Parchment-warm — a manuscript page tone, faint warm spotting at the corners, the suggestion of a desk in soft daylight. The five elf profiles sit in a horizontal frieze behind the bold word, painted with edges that fade into the parchment.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8, Ink #16110D, Gold #D4A24C, Crimson #B23A2A
Composition:    Asymmetric. Bold word slightly right-of-centre; five elf profiles arranged behind it in a horizontal band, each one occupying roughly equal space, with the central drow profile slightly larger as an anchor. Gilt-leaf border ornament in the top-left corner as the genre accent.
Lighting:       Warm morning light from frame-left, gentle and even. The five profiles each carry their subtype's signature lighting (cool on the high elf, warm on the wood elf, dramatic on the drow, watery on the sea elf, gold on the eladrin) but harmonised within the overall warm wash.
Mood:           Scholarly comparative anatomy. The kind of plate you'd see in an illuminated bestiary, but rendered with editorial gravitas.
Style:          oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges, Frazetta + Klimt + medieval illumination, multi-subject composition handled with restraint
Negative:       no skulls, no wax seals, no blackletter, no AI-cliché purple-orange dual rim light, no glowing eyes, no fantasy poses with raised weapons, no overly stylized anime cel shading on the cover (this is the painterly register), no garish saturated colour clash between the five subtypes, no dragons, no lens flares, no sparkles
