# Image prompts — Commercial licensing for commissioned art

Three prompts. Cover (16:9), inline mid-article (4:5 portrait), inline close (1:1 square). Follow `IMAGE-STYLE-GUIDE.md` — Cartographer's Hand, oil-painting brushwork, palette is Parchment / Ink / Gold / Crimson with Indigo used sparingly.

The cover accent for this evergreen is **a parchment scroll with an illuminated initial**, with the bold word **"RIGHTS"** or **"LICENSE"** as the typography anchor.

---

### Cover image (16:9, 1920×1080)

Subject:        A half-unrolled parchment scroll lying across an oak desk in candlelight. The scroll bears an illuminated capital initial — a giant gilded "R" rendered in medieval manuscript style, with a tiny painted figure (a half-occluded character portrait, three-quarter view, holding a small mahl stick) tucked inside the negative space of the letter. A wax-free brass weight pins the top corner of the scroll. A small inkwell and quill sit at the desk's lower-right edge, slightly out of focus.
Typography:     The bold word "RIGHTS" set in Cinzel 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, color Ink #16110D with subtle gilded inner-glow on the strokes (suggesting gold leaf hand-applied to the letter). Word occupies ~40% of canvas, sits in the upper-right third, with the painted figure peeking from behind the descender of the R. The illuminated capital "R" on the scroll itself is a separate decorative element in the lower-left third, in Gold #D4A24C with Crimson #B23A2A flourishes.
Kicker line:    "A FIELD GUIDE TO COMMISSIONING ART" set in Inter uppercase 600, letter-spacing 0.15em, color Crimson #B23A2A. Sits above the bold word "RIGHTS".
Accent line:    60×3px gold accent bar in #D4A24C directly under the kicker.
Background:     Parchment-warm — a candlelit study at the end of a long evening. Desk surface is rich oak. Background falls into deep shadow with a single hint of bookshelf in the far darkness. Warm key light from camera-left, just out of frame (the candle is implied, not shown).
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (scroll), Ink #16110D (deep shadow + headline), Gold #D4A24C (illuminated capital, accent bar, candlelight warmth), Crimson #B23A2A (kicker, flourish on the manuscript initial), Cream #FAF4E2 (subtle highlight on the scroll edge).
Composition:    Asymmetric. Scroll runs diagonally lower-left to upper-right. Typography sits upper-right with the painted figure half-occluded by the R. The eye travels from the kicker → bold word → painted figure → down the diagonal of the scroll to the illuminated capital → out to the inkwell. Plenty of breathing room around the typography. The painted figure inside the R is small but intentional — a curiosity hook, not a focal subject.
Lighting:       Single warm candle key from camera-left, falling across the scroll. Deep shadow on the right edge. Gold leaf catches micro-highlights. Cool ambient fill is very subtle — not enough to compete with the candle warmth.
Mood:           A scrivener's desk at the moment a commission contract gets sealed. Quiet, deliberate, scholarly. The painted figure inside the R hints at the painting the license refers to.
Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork on the painted figure, hand-lettered illuminated capital with real gold-leaf texture. Soft edges. Frazetta + Klimt + medieval illumination. The scroll itself is painted (not a texture overlay) with visible fibers and a slight curl at the edge.
Negative:       no wax seals, no skulls, no blackletter/Old English, no AI-cliché purple-orange dual rim lighting, no lens flares, no sparkles, no stock fantasy poses, no faux-parchment Photoshop textures, no dragons, no d20 dice, no scrollwork ornaments, no quill-and-scroll clipart energy, no overly-rendered armor on the painted figure, no glowing eyes, no beauty-filter face on the painted figure.

### Inline mid-article (4:5 portrait, 1080×1350)

Sits after H2 #3 ("The indie publisher tier") or H2 #4 ("The full commercial license").

Subject:        A painted character portrait — a writer-protagonist type, late thirties, holding a small bound novella with their own face on the cover. The painting-within-a-painting is the same character at a smaller scale, suggesting a book cover. The character looks down at the book in their hands with a quiet, satisfied expression. Wears a simple traveling cloak in muted indigo over a cream shirt. No armor, no fantasy gear — they read as a working creator rather than an adventurer.
Typography:     None. This is a quiet inline image.
Background:     A bookshop or printer's shop interior. Soft natural light from a window off camera-right. Stacks of unbound signatures on a wooden counter in the soft-focus background. Deep warm shadow in the upper-left corner.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (shirt, paper), Ink #16110D (deep background), Gold #D4A24C (book endpapers and warm window light), Crimson #B23A2A (a small ribbon bookmark hanging from the novella), Indigo #2C3E5C (cloak — used once, exactly as the style guide advises).
Composition:    Centered subject, three-quarter view, head and upper torso plus hands holding the book. Breathing room top and bottom. Eye line falls to the book, drawing the viewer's gaze to the painting-within-the-painting.
Lighting:       Single key from camera-right, naturalistic window light. Cool ambient fill from the bookshop interior. Warm bounce off the parchment shirt.
Mood:           The quiet moment a creator holds their own finished work for the first time. Satisfied, not triumphant. The painting and the book it appears on are the same image — making the licensing relationship visible without spelling it out.
Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, soft edges, painterly skin tones over a green underpainting on the figure. The book in their hands is rendered slightly more finished than the rest of the piece, drawing the eye.
Negative:       no typography overlay, no glowing magical elements, no fantasy armor, no weapons, no dragons, no purple-orange dual rim lighting, no skulls, no obvious "writer cliché" props like a feathered quill held aloft, no laptop or modern devices, no over-rendered face, no beauty filter, no sparkles.

### Inline close (1:1 square, 1080×1080)

Sits before the final CTA. Detail crop. Negative space dominates.

Subject:        A close detail of a hand pressing a signet ring into a small puddle of warm wax on the corner of a parchment contract. The wax is Crimson #B23A2A, the ring is gold, the hand is mid-press — caught at the instant of contact, not after. Only the lower forearm, the hand, the ring, and the wax are in frame. The rest of the contract trails out of the bottom of the crop.
Typography:     None.
Background:     Deep Ink #16110D upper half — falling into shadow. Lower half is the parchment surface in soft warm tones.
Palette:        Parchment #F4ECD8 (contract surface), Ink #16110D (shadow), Gold #D4A24C (signet ring, ring's reflected light on the wax), Crimson #B23A2A (wax).
Composition:    Strong diagonal — the forearm enters from upper-right, the hand and ring sit center-low, the wax and parchment edge run lower-left to lower-right. Large negative space upper-left.
Lighting:       Single warm candle key from camera-left. The wax catches a soft highlight. The ring has a single gilded micro-highlight. Deep shadow under the hand.
Mood:           The closing chord. The license is signed. The conversation is settled. Quiet, deliberate, a sense of agreement rather than ceremony.
Style:          Oil painting, visible brushwork, painterly hand anatomy — knuckles, tendons, slight wrinkle on the back of the hand. The wax has the slight glossy wet-edge of fresh seal wax, painted not photographed.
Negative:       no detailed seal imprint design (the ring is just pressing — what's stamped doesn't matter), no skulls, no blackletter, no overly-ornate ring engraving, no sparkles, no smoke trails, no AI-cliché magical glow, no second hand in frame, no quill in frame, no typography, no dust particles.
