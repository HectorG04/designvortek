FAQ

Every question, answered

Pricing, process, revisions, rights, refunds. If you've thought it, someone's asked it — and we've answered it here.

Pricing & payment

6 questions
How much does a commission cost?

Commissions range from $80 for a single VTT token to $600+ for a full party portrait. Character art starts at $180. See the full pricing breakdown.

Why flat-rate instead of hourly?

Hourly billing punishes care. You shouldn't pay more because we polished for an extra two hours. Fixed scope, fixed price, every time.

How does payment work?

25% deposit holds your slot (refundable until first sketch). Remaining 75% on delivery. All payments through Stripe.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes — NPC packs of 5+ get a per-piece discount. VTT tokens in bulk drop to $60 each at 8+. Custom retainer arrangements available for frequent collaborators.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, and ACH transfers through Stripe. We don't accept crypto or PayPal.

Are taxes included in the quoted price?

For US clients, no — sales tax (if applicable) is added at checkout based on your state. For international clients, the quoted price is the final price.

Process & timeline

5 questions
How long does a commission take?

Character portraits: 7–14 days. VTT tokens: 3–7 days. Party portraits & NPC packs: 2–4 weeks. Custom: by scope. Rush available for +$50 on most services.

What happens after I submit a brief?

Within 48 hours we review and send a fixed quote + timeline. You approve, pay 25% deposit, and we begin. Sketches typically land within a week.

Will I get updates during the painting?

Yes — sketches, color blocks, and paint progress shared every 3 days. You'll never wonder where things stand.

Can I request a rush?

Yes — +$50 moves you to the front of the queue with a 3-day turnaround on Character and Token services. Reach out before booking to confirm we have rush capacity.

What if I need to delay the project?

One pause of up to 30 days is fine — life happens. Beyond that we'll reschedule into a future slot when you're ready.

Revisions & quality

4 questions
How many revisions are included?

Sketch revisions are unlimited — we get the foundation right before paint. Two paint-stage revisions are baked into every Deluxe-tier commission. Premium tier includes three.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

In 500+ commissions, this has happened twice. If the piece is genuinely off the brief, we'll refund the balance beyond the 25% deposit. Read the full refund policy.

Can I get extra revisions?

Yes — +$40 buys an additional paint revision round.

Do you use AI in any part of the process?

No. Every piece is hand-painted from scratch by a human. No AI generation, no auto-fills, no traced outputs. That's the entire point.

Rights & licensing

4 questions
Can I use the art commercially?

Personal use (prints, social, character sheet) is included. Commercial licensing — books, merch, streaming, paid Patreon — is $150 per piece, or bulk arrangements for larger projects.

Can you post my commission on your portfolio?

By default, yes — this is how new clients find us. You can opt out at any point before delivery. Sensitive or NSFW commissions are never posted.

Can I commission a copyrighted character?

For personal use, we usually do fan art — your character sheet, your wall. For commercial use of someone else's IP, we'd need written permission from the rights holder.

Who owns the original file?

You own the rendered piece and the rights described above. We retain the right to display it in our portfolio (unless you opt out) and the underlying brush stamps, references, and processes.

Cancellations & refunds

3 questions
What's your refund policy?

Before first sketch: 100% refundable. After first sketch: deposit covers the sketch work. After paint begins: pro-rated by stage completed. Full refund policy.

Can I cancel a commission mid-project?

Yes — we'll pro-rate based on stage. You keep what's been delivered (sketches, color blocks) and we settle the balance.

What if you have to cancel?

In four years we've never canceled on a client. If we ever had to, you'd receive a full refund regardless of stage completed.

Still wondering?

Couldn't find your answer?

Send us a note. We usually reply within 48 hours, and the answer often becomes a new FAQ entry.
