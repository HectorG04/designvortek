Logo system · 01

Icon mark

The Vortek — a logarithmic spiral terminating in a quill stroke, inscribed in a compass circle. Three variants for three scales: solid for favicons, line for default, detailed for hero.

SOLID · ≤24px
LINE · default
DETAILED · ≥96px

Construction. 64×64 grid · 56×56 safe area · primary stroke 3.2px · secondary stroke 1.6–2.6px · rounded caps on spiral terminations · flat caps on frame · optical center sits ~0.5px above geometric center.

Mini variant · favicon-only sub-mark

A second, reductive mark for sizes where the full spiral collapses into mush. Single 3/4-revolution sweep with an inward quill terminus + the eye. Use only at 16–24px favicon scale and inside the embroidery / pin masters. Everywhere else, ship the primary line variant.

Modes & geometry

Construction

The icon mark is built on a 64×64 grid with a 56×56 safe area. Circle frame at radius 26. Spiral interior sits inside the safe area.

Grid: 20px cells on a 320×320 preview (5px on 64-unit canvas).
Bounds: 64×64 viewBox (solid dashed).
Safe: 56×56 — no element exits this zone (red dashed).
Frame: circle r=26 stroke 2.4–3.2px.
Spiral: logarithmic, ~1.5 revolutions, stroke 2.8–3.2px, rounded terminus.
Center dot: r=1.4 filled, optical centering ~0.5px above geometric.

Modes & geometry

Clear space

X = cap-height of "D" in DESIGN. Minimum clear space on all four sides = 1X. Preferred = 2X. No element may enter this zone.

Modes & geometry

Don't

Eight ways to break the mark. Avoid all of them.

Drop shadows

Never apply shadow, glow, bevel, or 3D.

Rotate

Never tilt the mark (except loading spinner).

Stretch

Never distort proportions.

Off-palette

Never apply colours outside the palette.

Gradients

The mark is single-colour. Never gradient.

Replace a letter

Defer to a designer (production-format conversion)

From the SVG masters, an external designer can produce the remaining formats: .fig / .ai source files, .eps for legacy print vendors, .dst + .pes embroidery (≤2 thread colors: Madeira Gold 1071 + Madeira Ink 1000), Pantone 871 C foil-stamp plates. The SVG is authoritative geometry.
