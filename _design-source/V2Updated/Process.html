How it works

From idea to artwork in four steps

Predictable, communicative, no surprises. You always know what's happening and when — here's exactly how each commission unfolds.

01

Discuss

Send us a brief through the commission form — or just an email. Tell us about the character, the campaign, the references that capture the vibe. We read carefully, ask the right follow-up questions, and only then start sketching ideas.

Usually 24–48 hours to get back to you with thoughts.

02

Quote

Within 48 hours of your brief, you get a fixed quote — not an estimate. Scope, timeline, total. A 25% deposit holds your slot (fully refundable until first sketch). The remaining 75% lands on delivery.

No hourly games. No surprises. The price you approve is the price you pay.

03

Create

Sketch first — we get the pose, composition, and likeness right before any paint touches the canvas. Sketch revisions are unlimited. From there: color blocks, then final paint. Two paint-stage revisions baked into every commission.

Updates every 3 days. You see the work as it grows.

04

Deliver

Final files at 4K resolution — PNG, JPG, and a transparent-background version. Layered PSD optional. Yours to print, frame, share, treasure. We follow up two weeks later to make sure you love it.

Most clients hang it on a wall. Some have it tattooed.

Timeline
Average turnaround — 7 to 14 days

From brief approval to final delivery. Tokens are faster (3–7 days). Party portraits and NPC packs take 2–4 weeks.

Ready to begin?

Three minutes to brief. Quote within 48 hours. First sketch within a week.
